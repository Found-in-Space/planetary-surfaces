# Black Marble: 2016 colour composite

Build NASA's Black Marble 2016 **colour maps** from all eight full-resolution
500 m RGB GeoTIFF sheets. The product preserves NASA's rendered nighttime
composite and its background; it is not a calibrated radiance product or a
lights-only emission mask. Its historical observation period differs from the
Blue Marble July 2004 companion.

See the [paired Earth workflow](../README.md) for source admission, durable
storage, full-pyramid commands, disk budget, credits and S3 publication handoff.
The checked-in acquisition specifications pin the complete source files and
SHA-256 checksums admitted after acquisition and review on 17 October 2026.
